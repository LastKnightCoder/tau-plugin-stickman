# Stickman

Place stick figures on the Whiteboard to illustrate actions, emotions, work scenarios, and team relationships, bringing flowcharts and diagrams to life. Choose from three styles (Classic, Bean, and Pictogram), use monochrome or accent colors, and flip figures horizontally.

## Getting Started

1. Install **Stickman** from the Plugin Center, enable it on the **Installed** tab, and click **Restart now** on the banner at the top of the Plugins page.
2. Open a Whiteboard, select **Figure** under **Extension Tools**, pick a pose, and click on the canvas to place it.
3. Select a figure to change its pose, style, color scheme, or horizontal flip from the property bar.

## What You Can Do

- Drag any corner handle to scale proportionally, and connect figures to other shapes with arrows.
- Follows the Whiteboard's drawing style by default, or switch individually to a hand-drawn look and adjust stroke roughness under **Stroke**.
- Newly placed figures remember your last chosen style and color scheme.
- Supports copy, undo, redo, and exports cleanly alongside the rest of the Whiteboard image.

## FAQ

**What happens to figures if I disable the plugin?** They appear as placeholders until you re-enable the plugin, with nothing lost.

**Why did my style preferences change after switching libraries?** Each library remembers its own preferences independently.

## Tips

- Restart Tau after enabling, disabling, or updating this plugin—the Plugins page will prompt you with **Restart now**.
